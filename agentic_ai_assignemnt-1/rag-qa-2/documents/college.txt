College Information

The college offers B.Tech programs in Computer Science, Electronics, Electrical Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, and Civil Engineering.

The B.Tech Computer Science program focuses on programming, data structures, algorithms, databases, operating systems, computer networks, artificial intelligence, machine learning, and web development.

The normal duration of a B.Tech program is four years, divided into eight semesters.

Students must complete academic courses, laboratory work, assignments, examinations, and projects.

The final year includes a major project that allows students to apply their technical knowledge to a practical problem.
